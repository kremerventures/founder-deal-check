Founder Deal Check

Use this when someone gives you a possible investment amount and valuation.

Before the meeting

Set your assumptions:

  • Target exit
  • Future dilution

These are saved on this device.

During the meeting

Enter:

  • Raise
  • Valuation

What the results mean

  • Investor Gets — their ownership from this round
  • You at Exit — your estimated ownership after future dilution
  • Your Take — estimated payout at your saved exit value
Tip: Type "2" for $2M, "500k" for $500K, or "8.5" for $8.5M.
This is a quick conversation tool, not a full cap table model.
